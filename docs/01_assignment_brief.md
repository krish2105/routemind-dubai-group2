## What this means for us
- **A working dashboard is not required.** "Conceptual workflow or prototype". We build a small prototype because it is the best proof for "Application of Techniques", but the 7 slides must stand on their own.
- **Team Collaboration** is one of four criteria: all 7 members present a slide and own a deliverable (see `03_team_split.md`).
- **Presentation Skills:** engaging means one message and one visual per slide, a live or recorded demo, and tight timing.
- **GenAI disclosure:** every prompt is logged (`docs/prompt_logs/<Name>.md`), and GenAI-made content on slides is circled in red.
